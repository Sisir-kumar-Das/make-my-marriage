Add an entry whenever the owner approves a new departure, and check this list before reporting a "mismatch" in reviews or tests.

The cover editor and recovery states reuse the existing approved uploader rather than introduce another upload design. No public website is claimed by the cover subtitle.

15 November follow-up to #20: Sisir approved focusing the error after a failed cover reload while retaining the conflict lock, and transferring focus to the existing external session alert when a later outage makes the currently focused draft inert. ## 21 — Wedding workspace lifecycle, 15 November 2026

Sisir approved Admin deletion with typed DELETE, seven-day recovery, pending-invitation cancellation, membership reservation during recovery and account/profile preservation. Use the corrected Settings lifecycle danger card, popup and recovery terminal. Display only the real applicable terminal; do not render the mockup's simultaneous lifecycle gallery/sample data. Family Member/Viewer see the same terminal without Restore and with “An Admin can restore this wedding.” Expiry may precede asynchronous cleanup, so say cleanup is pending/in progress rather than falsely claiming completed purge. The recovery route sits outside the unavailable workspace and reuses the local brand header/logout action. Keep role labels truthful, deadline in IST and the reference typography/card tokens; reload/uncertain-result feedback uses existing controls without browser persistence. See ADR-052.

16 November follow-up: Sisir approved placing Reload with the applicable card's actions, explicit confirmation-input focus, trapped keyboard navigation and in-dialog uncertain-result errors. Pending actions focus the dialog container; exact retries remain locked to their original request. The countdown uses the server time sample, with “Checking current recovery status” when the animated sample reaches zero; it never hides Restore or announces expiry based on the device calendar. Match the reference's 24 px Delete / 40 px Restore horizontal padding, 15 px body text, control typography and icons, including their phone sizes. No change to `/profile` access or root redirects was approved or made.
